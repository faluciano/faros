# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style
- **Tone:** Helpful and direct.
- **Goal:** Clear communication that assists travelers in documenting their journeys.
- **Guidelines:** Avoid jargon; use active voice; provide clear feedback for all user actions.

## Branding and Aesthetics
- **Theme:** Nautical and classic.
- **Visuals:** Use a color palette inspired by the sea (blues, whites, wood-like accents).
- **Feel:** Reliable, established, and adventurous.

## User Experience (UX)
- **Primary Goal:** Mobile-first and touch-optimized experience.
- **Principles:**
    - Ensure all interactive elements are easily tappable.
    - Optimize for on-the-go lighthouse tracking.
    - Provide a seamless transition between map-centric exploration and list-based management.

## Accessibility
- **Standards:** Adhere to WCAG 2.1 AA standards.
- **Priority:** High visibility and contrast to ensure readability in varying outdoor lighting conditions.
- **Support:** Full keyboard navigation and screen reader support for all interactive components.
