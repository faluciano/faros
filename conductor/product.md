# Initial Concept
Web app to track visited lighthouses around the world with a map to display visited points.

# Product Guide

## Vision
Faros is the definitive platform for lighthouse enthusiasts and social travelers to document, explore, and share their nautical journeys. We aim to be the most accurate and precise global resource for lighthouse tracking.

## Goals
- Provide the most comprehensive and high-precision database of lighthouses worldwide.
- Foster a social community where travelers can share their visits and find new destinations.
- Deliver a reliable and accessible experience through a modern Progressive Web App (PWA).

## Features

### Current
- Interactive global lighthouse map (using Pigeon-Maps/MapLibre).
- Detailed Lighthouse Specifications: High-precision metadata including height, year built, light characteristics, and historical descriptions.
- User authentication and social connections (Clerk).
- Personal tracking and wishlist management.
- Friend discovery and shared maps.

### Upcoming / Priority
- **PWA Enhancements:** Improving offline support and mobile-like experience on all devices.
- **Future:** Lighthouse visit photo uploads and social sharing.

## Target Audience
- **Lighthouse Enthusiasts:** Dedicated travelers who seek out and document lighthouses.
- **Social Travelers:** People who enjoy mapping their travels and sharing with friends.

## Unique Value Proposition
- **Unrivaled Data Precision:** Faros distinguishes itself through the accuracy and depth of its lighthouse data, making it the primary tool for serious enthusiasts.
